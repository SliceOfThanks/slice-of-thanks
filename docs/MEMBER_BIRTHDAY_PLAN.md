# Member birthday pizza (draft, 2026-10-25)

Status: approved by Stacey 2026-10-25. Name, note and 30-day sharing approved. Whole Pie gift is Option A, with a new birthday topper designed every year. Not built yet.

Slice of Thanks sends its own members a birthday pizza. It is a special pizza nobody can make in the Kitchen.

## Asking for the birthday
- Month and day only, never the year.
- Asked in three places, all optional:
  - the onboarding checklist, as a "Your birthday" step
  - Settings, You card
  - a small card on Home that asks once
- Members can turn birthday pizzas off in Settings.
- A birthday can only be changed once every 12 months, so nobody moves it to get the gift again.

## The pizza: Gerald's Birthday Special
- Made only for members, and only on their birthday.
- Toppings: star and heart pepperoni, sprinkles and birthday candles. Sprinkles and candles are new toppings that only appear on this pizza.
- Their first name is spelled in pepperoni.
- The box is the Birthday Bash box. The lid sticker says "Happy Birthday" and the business name on the lid says "Gerald and Potato".
- The confetti is new and only used here: birthday candles and party hats.
- Potato delivers it wearing a party hat.
- Inside the lid, Gerald's note: "Happy birthday, [first name]! The whole kitchen made this one just for you. Potato ate one slice. Sorry." It is signed "Gerald, Head Chef, and Potato, Head of Delivery".
- Gerald's receipt has a special line: "Birthday Special. On the house."

## When it arrives
- 8 AM in the member's time zone on their birthday, by email, from Slice of Thanks.
- It uses none of the member's emails.

## The gift under the pizza (revealed after the sixth slice)
- A Slice members: 14 days of The Whole Pie, with no card. They tap "Unlock my 14 days". They have 30 days after their birthday to claim it, and the 14 days start when they tap.
- Whole Pie members: 100 bonus emails for 30 days, plus that year's birthday box topper, theirs to keep. A new birthday topper is designed every year (for example 2026: party hat cake, 2027: something new), so members collect them.
- Pizza Party members: a team extra once seats are built.

## Bonus: share the Birthday Special
For 30 days after their birthday, the member can send the Birthday Special to people in their own People list. This spreads the word, and every pizza they send shows Slice of Thanks.

## Rules
- One birthday pizza and one gift per account every 12 months.
- The account must be at least 14 days old.
- Privacy policy: one line saying Slice of Thanks keeps a member's birthday (month and day only) to send a birthday gift.

## Build order (after launch)
1. The plan switch (shared with the free trial and the Treat Jar prize).
2. The birthday field, the Home card, and the onboarding step.
3. The Birthday Special pizza, the new toppings, the confetti, and Potato's party hat.
4. The daily job that sends it at 8 AM, using the same system as date pizzas.
5. The gift and the "Unlock my 14 days" button.
About a day in total.
