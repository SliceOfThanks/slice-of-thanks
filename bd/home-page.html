FAQs

A few things before you dig in.

Is this actual pizza?

It’s a digital thank-you pizza delivered by email. All the appreciation, considerably less grease.

What does the recipient get?

An email with a link to their pizza. They open the box, read your note, and “eat” it one slice at a time. They do not need an account.

Can I include a gift?

Yes. You can add your own gift or coupon code. You supply the gift; we supply the pizza-shaped presentation.

Can I start for free?

Yes. A Slice is free forever, and every new account gets 14 days of The Whole Pie first. No card needed. See Pricing.

What happens after the 14 days?

You stay on A Slice for free unless you upgrade. Everything you made stays saved. Pizzas that went out on their own now wait for you to send.

Can they send one back?

Yes. Every pizza has a Send one back button. You show up in their People already filled in, so it takes them a minute.
