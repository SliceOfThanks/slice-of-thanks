Meet your critic

This is Gerald

Gerald is a raccoon and the head critic at Slice of Thanks. He spent ten years eating leftover crusts behind a family pizzeria, and now he reviews every pizza before it goes out the door.

  • Rates in: slices, 1 to 5
  • Favorite topping: whatever fell on the floor
  • Known for: his bow tie and very strong opinions
  • House rule: any pizza made with care gets at least 3 slices

His review prints on a receipt taped to every box, so the people you send pizzas to meet him too. His delivery partner is Potato, who brings every pizza to the door. You can meet her in the Crew tab.
