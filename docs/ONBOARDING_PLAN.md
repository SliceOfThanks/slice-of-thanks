# Onboarding plan (draft, 2026-10-25)

Status: approved by Stacey 2026-10-25. Not built yet.

## How the free Pro trial works
- Everyone signs up on A Slice in BD, with no card.
- On their first sign in, Slice of Thanks switches on The Whole Pie for 14 days. This is the same plan switch planned for the birthday gift, run by our server. BD is not changed, so nobody is billed.
- During the trial they get every Whole Pie feature and 50 trial emails.
- When the trial ends they drop back to A Slice unless they upgrade. Nothing they made is lost. Pro items stay saved and show a Pro tag until they upgrade.
- One trial per account.
- People who pick The Whole Pie at sign up go through BD checkout as they do today, and get the same Pro onboarding.

## What they see
1. A welcome screen the first time the Kitchen opens. Gerald and Potato say hello, and the screen says: "Your 14 days of The Whole Pie start now. No card needed."
2. A "Get set up" checklist card at the top of Home: "3 of 9 done". Each step has a Do it button that opens the right screen, and every step can be skipped. The card folds away and can be dismissed. Progress is saved to the account.
3. Finishing the checklist plays confetti and finds a Treat Jar egg: "Fully set up, +20 Treats".

## Steps on the Pro trial (and paid Whole Pie)
1. Name and business: your name, business name, and send as you or as your business.
2. Where replies go: your email, filled in from your login.
3. Logo and brand colors: upload a logo and pick two colors. Can skip.
4. Your signature pizza: pick a premade, or make your own in the Kitchen.
5. Send a test pizza to yourself, so you see exactly what people get. This one does not use an email.
6. Add your people: upload a CSV, or add three people with birthdays and tags.
7. Connect your calendar: Calendly or TidyCal. Choose "send my signature pizza after meetings" or "I pick the meetings".
8. Turn on Gerald never forgets a date. Pick sends on its own or waits for you, and pick a note style.
9. Your birthday, month and day only, optional. Slice of Thanks sends you Gerald's Birthday Special (docs/MEMBER_BIRTHDAY_PLAN.md).

Optional extras after the 9 steps:
- Make a box preset with your button and a gift code.
- Try Send to a group with a small list.

## Steps on A Slice (after the trial, or anyone who stays free)
1. Name and business.
2. Where replies go.
3. Your signature pizza: pick a premade or make one.
4. Send a test pizza to yourself.
5. Add your people, by adding them one at a time with birthdays.
6. Try Hire Gerald: let him make a pizza for someone.
7. Turn on Gerald never forgets a date. On A Slice, pizzas wait for you to send.
8. Your birthday, month and day only, optional.

Each Pro step a free member sees shows one line on what it does, with a "Try it with The Whole Pie" button.

## Around the end of the trial
- 3 days before the end: a card on Home and one email saying "3 days left of The Whole Pie", with what they would keep by upgrading.
- On the last day: "Your trial ends today."
- After it ends: a card on Home saying what changed (logo, button, gift, automations, group sends) and an Upgrade button.
- Meeting automations and date pizzas that send on their own switch to "wait for you" automatically, so nothing breaks.

## Build order (after launch)
1. The plan switch (shared with the birthday gift and the Treat Jar prize).
2. The welcome screen and the Get set up checklist, with progress saved on the account.
3. Free test sends to yourself.
4. The end-of-trial cards and email.
About a day in total.

## Decisions (Stacey, 2026-10-25)
1. Trial length: 14 days. The BD trial on The Whole Pie and Pizza Party is also 14 days, matching the Join page.
2. No card for the trial.
3. 50 emails during the trial.
4. Test sends to yourself are free, up to 5.
5. +20 Treats for finishing the checklist.
